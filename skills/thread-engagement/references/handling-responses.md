# Handling responses

After a reply goes live, people answer. Use these rules. The owner posts every reply, so these rules guide the owner and the drafts for follow-ups.

## Someone thanks you

No reply is needed. A like or an upvote is enough. Do not write "glad to help".

## Someone asks a follow-up question

Answer once, in two to four sentences. Answer only the question. If the question asks for a link and the community allows links, give the link. If the community does not allow links, say the name and stop.

## Someone asks "is this an ad?" or "do you work there?"

Answer yes in one sentence if the answer is yes. Repeat the affiliation. Offer the plain facts. Example: "Yes, I work on it, so I am not neutral. The free plan covers 5 clients, which is what most solo people need."

Never deny the connection. Never delete the reply to avoid the question.

## Someone disagrees

If the point is fair, say so and add the missing detail. If the point is wrong, give one fact. Do not argue a second round. Leave the thread.

## Someone is hostile

Do not reply. Do not report unless the comment breaks the rules and harms others. Leave the thread.

## A moderator removes the reply

1. Stop. Do not repost.
2. Do not send an angry message.
3. Read the removal reason and the community rules. Log the reason in the app (reply status rejected, with the reason).
4. Lower the pitch level for that community to 0 until the owner reads the rules again.
5. If the moderator asks a question, answer once, politely.

## A moderator warns or bans the account

1. Stop all replies in that community.
2. Do not use another account to get around the ban. That breaks the site rules and puts every account at risk.
3. Tell the owner. Offer to send a polite message to ask for the rule detail, and let the owner decide.

## The reply gets downvoted

A score of 0 or 1 after a day is normal. A score below minus 3 means the reply missed the tone or the question. Read the comments under it. Use the finding to adjust the next draft. Do not delete and repost.

## The thread gets locked

Stop. Mark the lead as closed.

## Follow-up check

Check each posted reply once, 24 hours after posting. Record the score and whether it is still visible. The app stores this outcome so the next run learns which communities and which reply shapes work.
