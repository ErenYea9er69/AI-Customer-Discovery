# Warm-up plan

The owner links an account they already use. A real history makes replies credible and keeps the account safe. This plan uses the account's real numbers, so an old account may skip early steps. The app computes the stage from account age, comment karma and the share of recent comments that mention the product.

Thresholds are the app's own defaults. Reddit publishes no cutoffs. Vendor guides suggest two to four weeks of normal commenting and 100 or more comment karma before a first product mention. Communities set their own minimums.

## Stages

| Stage | Account signs | Max pitch level | Daily cap | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Build | Under 30 days or under 100 comment karma | 0 | 2 | Comment with real help in the target communities. No product mention. |
| 1. Trusted | 30 days or more, 100 karma or more | 1 | 3 | Describe approaches and kinds of tools. No brand. |
| 2. Established | 90 days or more, 300 karma or more | 2 | 5 | Mention the product once with affiliation where rules allow. |
| 3. Veteran | 180 days or more, 500 karma or more, history in the community | 3 | 5 | Link only when asked and allowed. |

Drop one stage when the share of the last 25 comments that mention the product is above 10 percent.

## Weekly rhythm for an account at stage 0 or 1

- Week 1 to 2: 5 to 10 helpful comments a week in 2 to 3 target communities. No product. Read the rules. Let the account show a real interest.
- Week 3 to 4: Continue. Answer follow-ups. Start level 1 replies where a tool request fits.
- After week 4 with 100 karma: allow level 2 in communities that permit it, one reply at most per 9 other comments.

## Rules for all stages

1. Comment on topics the owner knows. A short real answer beats a long generic one.
2. Spread activity over several days. Vary the time of day.
3. Do not post in more than 3 communities the same day.
4. Do not reply to a post the owner already replied to.
5. Keep other activity natural. The account should show interests outside the product's field when the owner has them.
6. Never ask others to upvote.

## What the app records

- The account snapshot: age, comment karma, link karma, fetch time.
- The product mention ratio of recent published replies.
- The stage and the allowed level.
- The community rule acknowledgement per community.

## When to pause

Pause for 7 days after a removal for self-promotion, after a moderator warning, or when the account's recent replies all sit at a score of 0 or below.
