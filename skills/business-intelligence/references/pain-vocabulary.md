# Pain vocabulary

Pain vocabulary is the list of phrases customers type when they have the problem and have not heard of the product. Search queries, lead scoring and reply writing all use it. A weak list gives weak search.

## How to build the list

1. Start from each problem in problems_solved.
2. Remove the solution. "Automated invoice reminders" becomes "late invoices".
3. Add the feeling. "Late invoices" becomes "hate chasing clients for payment".
4. Add the situation. "Hate chasing clients for payment" becomes "client is 45 days late and I do not want to be rude".
5. Write each phrase in 3 to 9 words, in the first person where it fits.
6. Make variants: a complaint, a question, a request for a tool, a request for a script.

## Word rules

- Use the words of the community, not the words of the vendor. People write "chase", "nag", "ghost", "net 30", "late payer". They do not write "accounts receivable optimization".
- Keep contractions and plain verbs. "can't keep up with" beats "unable to maintain".
- Do not include the product name or the category label the vendor uses.
- Skip marketing nouns: solution, platform, ecosystem, workflow, suite, stack.

## Worked examples

Product: invoice reminder tool for freelancers.

| Feature side | Customer side phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Automated payment reminders | how do you politely chase late invoices |
| Overdue dashboard | I lose track of who still owes me money |
| Reminder templates | what do you say in a payment reminder email |
| Late fee settings | should I charge late fees to a small client |

Product: backlog triage for engineering teams.

| Feature side | Customer side phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Duplicate detection | so many duplicate bug reports every week |
| Auto labeling | tired of labeling github issues by hand |
| Priority scoring | how do you decide what goes in the sprint |
| Slack alerts | we missed a critical bug because nobody saw it |

Product: meal planning app for people with allergies.

| Feature side | Customer side phrase |
| --- | --- |
| Allergen filters | cooking for a kid with a nut allergy is exhausting |
| Weekly plan | what do you eat when you cannot have dairy or eggs |
| Shopping list | label reading takes me an hour at the store |
| Recipe swaps | substitute for eggs in baking that actually works |

## Quality check

Read each phrase aloud. If a person would not say it in a post or a comment, rewrite it. If the phrase would fit an ad headline, rewrite it. Keep 8 to 15 phrases and drop duplicates in meaning.
